# Attribution and License Notice

The HTML fixtures in this directory (the 13 `.html` files under `earlier/` and their 13 counterparts under `later/`) contain excerpts of Cloudflare's AI Crawl Control documentation archived by the Internet Archive Wayback Machine in October 2025 and April 2026.

- **Authors**: Cloudflare, Inc. and Cloudflare Documentation contributors
- **Source**: [Cloudflare Documentation (`cloudflare/cloudflare-docs`)](https://github.com/cloudflare/cloudflare-docs#license-and-legal-notices)
- **License**: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Modifications**: Extracted the main article HTML (`<h1 id="_top">` and `sl-markdown-content`) from each exact Wayback Machine replay URL listed in `manifest.json`, preserving headings, paragraphs, lists, links, tables, `<aside>` callouts, `<starlight-tabs>` tab widgets, zoomable `<img>` images, inline `<code>`, and `<pre>` code blocks while removing site navigation, footers, Wayback replay toolbars, scripts, buttons, and SVGs. The embedded video iframe is removed, and absolute Wayback replay prefixes in link targets are restored to the original captured site URLs.

## Skipped pages

A Wayback Machine CDX query on the `developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control` prefix (October 2025: 2025-10-01 through 2025-10-31; April 2026: 2026-04-01 through 2026-04-30) found 13 page URLs with a successful HTML capture in both windows. Those 13 pairs feed the comparison fixtures in `earlier/` and `later/`, from the captures closest to 2025-10-02 and 2026-04-29, the capture dates of the first two pairs. Asset files (`.md`, `.xml`, `.txt`), query-string variants of a canonical page URL, and captures that replayed with a non-200 status or a non-HTML content type were excluded.
